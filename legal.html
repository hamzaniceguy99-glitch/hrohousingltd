What we provide

HRO Housing provides the provision of other accommodation and the letting and operating of its own or leased real estate by HRO HOUSING LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 12845578. The company shares a director and registered office with HRO ESTATES LTD, company number 11264086, which operates separately. IMPORTANT — LICENSING OF HOUSES IN MULTIPLE OCCUPATION: under the Housing Act 2004 a house in multiple occupation requires a licence from the local housing authority. Mandatory licensing applies where the property is occupied by five or more persons forming two or more households who share an amenity, and a local authority may in addition designate areas or property types subject to additional licensing or selective licensing. OPERATING A LICENSABLE PROPERTY WITHOUT A LICENCE IS AN OFFENCE which may attract an unlimited fine on conviction or a financial penalty imposed by the authority; IN ADDITION A RENT REPAYMENT ORDER MAY BE MADE REQUIRING REPAYMENT OF UP TO TWELVE MONTHS' RENT, on the application of a tenant or the local authority, AND NO NOTICE UNDER SECTION 21 OF THE HOUSING ACT 1988 MAY BE SERVED DURING A PERIOD IN WHICH THE PROPERTY IS REQUIRED TO BE LICENSED AND IS NOT. The company obtains the required licence before letting a licensable property, complies with the conditions attached to it including any limit on the number of occupants, and holds licences under [HMO LICENCE NUMBERS AND ISSUING AUTHORITY]. MANAGEMENT DUTIES: the manager of a house in multiple occupation is subject to the Management of Houses in Multiple Occupation (England) Regulations 2006, which require among other things that the manager's name, address and contact details be displayed in the property, that means of escape from fire be kept free from obstruction and in good order, that fire precautions be maintained, that the water supply and drainage, gas and electricity installations and common parts be maintained in repair and proper working order, and that living accommodation and shared amenities be kept in repair and clean. FIRE SAFETY: the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 applies to the common parts, requiring a fire risk assessment to be carried out and kept up to date, and the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022 impose further duties in relevant premises; detection, alarm and escape arrangements are provided in accordance with the assessment and any licence conditions. CONDITION: premises are let in a condition fit for human habitation as required by the Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018 and maintained accordingly, hazards being assessed by reference to the housing health and safety rating system. SAFETY DOCUMENTATION: annual gas safety checks by a Gas Safe registered engineer, an electrical installation condition report at intervals of not more than five years, smoke alarms on each storey and carbon monoxide alarms where required are provided, with copies given to occupiers. A valid energy performance certificate is held and the applicable minimum energy efficiency standard observed. SUPPORTED HOUSING: THE COMPANY IS NOT A REGISTERED PROVIDER OF SOCIAL HOUSING and does not claim to be, registration being a separate matter; where any accommodation is provided with support, the requirements applicable to such accommodation, including those introduced by the Supported Housing (Regulatory Oversight) Act 2023 and any local licensing scheme, are complied with. The company does not provide care, and any care service requiring registration with the Care Quality Commission is neither provided nor arranged. Applicants are assessed against consistent written criteria in accordance with the Equality Act 2010, and right to rent checks are carried out where required.
